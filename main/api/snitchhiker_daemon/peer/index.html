Module peer 

Source
Expand description

Who is on the other end of a unix socket, from facts only the kernel and root can establish, and whether it is one of Snitchhiker’s own client binaries.

Nothing a client sends counts: no tokens, no passwords. A client is trusted when the kernel says it runs a root-owned binary that root registered as trusted, with the client group that only that binary’s setgid bit gives, nobody tracing it, and memory no other process of its user may read.

end_with_process
Ends stream when the process of pidfd exits, even if another process holds the connection (it was passed on, or inherited): a client’s rights are its process’s, not its socket’s. Dropping the returned guard stops watching.
identify
Identifies the process connected to stream. The process is held by a pidfd, and checked to still be alive after everything was read, so its pid can’t have been reused by another process in between. The pidfd is returned: it tells when that process exits.
