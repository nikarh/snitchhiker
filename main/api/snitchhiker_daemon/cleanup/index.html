snitchhiker_daemon

Module cleanup 

Source
Expand description

snitchhikerd cleanup: what happens to the daemon’s tables after it stopped (the service runs it after every stop, ExecStopPost, with systemd’s SERVICE_RESULT saying how the stop went).

  • With fail = "open", run by hand, or with a configuration that doesn’t read: they are removed.
  • With fail = "closed", after a clean stop: they stay (the daemon kept them; nothing gets out until it is back).
  • With fail = "closed", after a crash: they stay for a restart, unless the daemon crashed CRASHES times in WINDOW: a daemon that can’t stay up mustn’t keep the machine offline. Then they are removed after each crash until the crashes are that far apart again.
