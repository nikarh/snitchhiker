Expand description

All present conditions must hold; a list means any of its items. Patterns are exact unless prefixed with glob: or re:. Ports are numbers or low-high ranges; protocols tcp, udp, icmp, icmpv6 or numbers.

Unspecified: the host only.

§session: Vec<String>

Processes (pid@start: the pid and its start time in clock ticks since boot) the rule is limited to, with everything they start: an agent’s session.

§container: Option<ContainerConditions>

A container’s identity: the rule then holds for containers only.

§vm: Option<VmConditions>

A VM’s: for VMs only.

§netns: Vec<String>

Names of network namespaces (ip netns).

§list: Vec<String>

Blocklists, by name: the destination’s name, a domain above it, or its address is on one of them.

§sha256: Vec<String>

SHA-256s of the executable (hex): the binary pinned.

§writable: Option<bool>

Whether a user other than root can change the executable; unset: either.
