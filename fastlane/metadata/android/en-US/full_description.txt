<p>Snitchhiker is an interactive application firewall for Linux: it asks before a program connects somewhere no rule
covers, and keeps the answer as a rule. This app is its remote control, for your workstations and your servers alike.</p>

<p>Pair the app with a machine running Snitchhiker by scanning its QR code, opening its link or typing its code. The
phone connects to it directly, or through your own relay when the machine is behind NAT or a firewall. Connections are
end-to-end encrypted, with keys only your devices and machines hold.</p>

<ul>
<li>Prompts as notifications, answered with Allow or Deny from the notification, or several at once in the app</li>
<li>Woken through UnifiedPush (with ntfy, for example, and your own server) when a prompt waits, and told when a machine goes offline and why</li>
<li>Rules by folder: search, edit, enable, move and delete them, one by one or together; import and export rules files</li>
<li>The connection history, live events, the Explorer (who connected where, how much), open connections</li>
<li>Explaining why a connection was allowed or blocked; checking the rules for mistakes; undoing changes</li>
<li>The system firewall, alerts and alert rules, the machine's settings</li>
<li>Rules proposed by AI agents, reviewed and approved or denied</li>
<li>Several machines side by side, each with its own devices and roles</li>
<li>An optional app lock, with your fingerprint or screen lock</li>
</ul>

<p>The app needs a machine running Snitchhiker: see the documentation for installing it.</p>
