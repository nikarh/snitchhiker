Netfilter over netlink: Snitchhiker’s nf_tables table and its NFQUEUE queues, without libnftables or libnetfilter_queue (both GPL, and libnftables reads every table’s sets before changing any).

Modules§

netlink
Netlink messages for the netfilter subsystems: a writer that builds them into a reused buffer, a parser that reads them in place, and a socket that sends a batch and collects the kernel’s answers.
nftables
Snitchhiker’s nf_tables table, written as netlink batches.
packet
The headers of a queued packet: addresses, protocol and ports, read in place.
queue
NFQUEUE: packets that the table’s queue rules hold until the daemon decides.
sockdiag
Closing a local socket by its addresses (sock_diag SOCK_DESTROY): how a rejected connection fails at once instead of timing out. The program’s connect() or next send returns ECONNABORTED.
